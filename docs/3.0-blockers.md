proof would be fabricated.

## Open blockers

### 1. Manual NLE QA

Kdenlive 26.08.2 is installed as a Flatpak (`org.kde.kdenlive`). Resolve and
Premiere are no longer part of the QA/install plan. Automated structure
validation passes, but a human must open the Kdenlive project and check titles,
colour, audio, B-roll, chapters, transitions, and clicks.

Required evidence: `docs/nle-qa.md` signed for Kdenlive, with a job id and the
exact export folder. `melt` is not installed; the optional MLT CLI smoke is
skipped.

### 2. Manual assistive-technology QA

Orca is installed and `DISPLAY=:20` exists, but no human has signed the
keyboard/screen-reader/zoom-200%/contrast checklist. Automated keyboard, focus,
narrow viewport, locale, and reduced-motion tests are green.

Required evidence: `docs/accessibility-qa.md` signed.

## Closed decision: voice cloning stays shell-only

No configured provider exposes a documented voice-clone endpoint. The decision
in [voice-clone.md](voice-clone.md) is final for 3.0: ship contracts, consent
metadata, profile storage, and status endpoints, but return
`capability_unavailable` from synthesis and keep the existing TTS/dubbing
fallback. This is a product decision, not a missing implementation.

## Resolved debt

The runner no longer merges ambient domain state. `artifacts`, `metadata`,
`shorts`, `selected_shorts`, `short_captions`, transcript changes, and speech
spans cross steps only through declared output ports. The runner keeps hashes,
cache bookkeeping, and warnings as diagnostics because they are not domain data.

## What is safe to claim

- 2.58.2 is a complete local release with checksums, SBOM, and a passing suite.
- Retakes v2 groups paraphrased takes from a normalised transcript, with
  deterministic guards for unique content and a review report.
- `llm.zoom` and `llm.style` are separate nodes; `llm.timeline` defaults to
  style/zoom only and closes gaps once after a batch. MP4 and NLE exports
  always receive a contiguous timeline.
- Transcript-span timelines and the noise filter remove VAD tails, coughs, and
  watermark hallucinations without cutting kept words.
- The 95% full-tree coverage gate is met with a reproducible report.
- 3.0 is blocked only by the two human QA sign-offs above.
- No artifact was pushed, published, or signed.
